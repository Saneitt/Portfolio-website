04 / HOW I BUILD

My way into
an idea.

A piece of software, a film, or something on the workbench starts the same way for me: understand it, give it a shape, and try it. AI is now central to how I build software—helping me explore approaches, prototype, and iterate efficiently.

?↗↻→
01

Understand the problem.

+

Ask what someone is trying to do, where the friction is, and what the system actually allows. I enjoy going deep enough to explain it clearly.

02

Give the idea a shape.

+

A flow, a storyboard, a working screen. Make the smallest useful version of the idea concrete enough to discuss and try.

03

Plan the work. Stay adaptable.

+

I like strategy, sequencing, and coordinating the moving pieces. A good plan makes it easier to notice what needs to change as the work develops.

04

Build efficiently with AI.

+

I use AI to explore implementation choices, turn ideas into working prototypes, and work through unfamiliar code. Clear context and focused tasks help me get useful results quickly. I review the code, test the important paths, and take responsibility for what I ship.

05

Build, explain, improve.

+

Follow the details through implementation, make the reasoning understandable, and use what the result teaches me in the next iteration.
